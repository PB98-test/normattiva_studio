# Art. 1857 - ***Norme applicabili***

Alle operazioni regolate in conto corrente si applicano le norme degli [articoli 1826](../../16%20-%20Capo%20XVI%20-%20Del%20conto%20corrente/art-1826-cc.md), [1829](../../16%20-%20Capo%20XVI%20-%20Del%20conto%20corrente/art-1829-cc.md) e [1832](../../16%20-%20Capo%20XVI%20-%20Del%20conto%20corrente/art-1832-cc.md).
